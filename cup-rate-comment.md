Many Cuban businesses need to operate in CUP (Peso Cubano) for accounting purposes and tax declarations. When the official exchange rate (for accounting) differs from USD rates, having accurate CUP conversion in Fedi would allow merchants to:
- Keep books in CUP as required by Cuban regulations
- Declare taxes with correct CUP valuations
- Maintain compliance while using modern Bitcoin/Lightning infrastructure

**2. USD Not Always Convenient**
There are scenarios where using USD in Cuba isn't practical for merchants:
- Local suppliers and workers are paid in CUP
- Some transactions legally require CUP denomination
- Cash flow management is easier when the app reflects real CUP values
- Customers often pay in CUP, not USD

**3. Fedi as a Business Tool in Cuba**
With correct CUP rates, Fedi becomes viable for:
- Small businesses managing daily transactions in CUP
- Freelancers receiving payments and needing CUP accounting
- Cooperatives requiring proper financial reporting in local currency
- Merchants bridging Bitcoin/Lightning with traditional Cuban accounting systems
